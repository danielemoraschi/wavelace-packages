# Changelog

What changed in @wavelace/embed, newest first. Wavelace's own changes, the app's and the site's, are at https://www.wavelace.com/changelog.

## 0.2.1

15 October 2026

- Added `CHANGELOG.md`, what changed in each version, and the release notes made from it.

## 0.2.0

14 October 2026

- Added the `maths` option: the plate as mathematics, `|x|` and `45°`, by default, or as the formula box takes it with `maths: false`.
- Added the presets `magnetic-field-line`, `spinning-drum-mode`, `orbiting-poles`, `quartic-well`, `enneper-surface` and `four-wing-attractor`.

## 0.1.0

28 September 2026

- Added `embedUrl`, `embedHtml`, `embedAttributes` and `embed`: an iframe of any Wavelace preset or formula, from a few options.
- Added `<Embed>` for React at `@wavelace/embed/react`.
